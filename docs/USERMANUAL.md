# EduSphere User Manual

Welcome to EduSphere! This platform is designed to make educational management seamless and intuitive for students, faculty, and administrators. This manual will guide you through the primary features of your respective portal.

---

## 👨‍🎓 Student Guide

As a student, your portal is your central hub for courses, materials, and assessments.

### 1. Dashboard Overview
Upon logging in, you will be greeted by the Student Dashboard. Here you can see:
*   **Enrolled Courses:** Quick access buttons for all your active courses.
*   **Recent Activity:** A timeline of recent announcements, new materials, and upcoming deadlines.
*   **Navigation Sidebar:** Easily jump between your courses, profile, and dashboard using the left-hand sidebar (or the hamburger menu on mobile).

### 2. Accessing Course Content
*   Click on any course card from your dashboard or sidebar to enter the **Course Manager**.
*   **Overview Tab:** Read the latest announcements from your instructor.
*   **Materials Tab:** View and download files (PDFs, documents) uploaded by your faculty for studying.

### 3. Taking Quizzes
*   Navigate to the **Quizzes Tab** within a course.
*   Click **"Take Quiz"** on an available quiz.
*   Answer Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) by selecting the ratio buttons.
*   For Descriptive questions, type your answer into the provided text box.
*   Click **"Submit Quiz"** when finished. *Note: MCQs are graded instantly, while descriptive answers will be marked as 'Pending Manual Review' until your instructor grades them.*

### 4. Submitting Assignments
*   Navigate to the **Assignments Tab** within a course.
*   View the assignment instructions, deadline, and download any attached reference files.
*   Click **"Submit Assignment"** and choose your file to upload.
*   Once graded by your instructor, you can return to this page to see your **Score** and read the **Faculty Feedback**.

## 👨‍🏫 Faculty Guide

As a faculty member, you have the tools to manage your courses, share resources, and evaluate student performance.

### 1. Course Management
*   Your Dashboard displays the courses assigned to you.
*   Click a course to enter the **Faculty Course Manager**.

### 2. Sharing Materials & Announcements
*   **Announcements Tab:** Use this to broadcast important messages to all students enrolled in your course.
*   **Materials Tab:** Upload syllabus documents, lecture slides, or reading materials. These are instantly available to your students.

### 3. Creating & Grading Quizzes
*   **Creating:** Go to the Quizzes tab and click "Create Quiz". Give it a title and add questions. You can mix auto-graded MCQs with manually-graded Descriptive questions. Set the correct answers for MCQs before publishing.
*   **Grading:** Once students submit, click "Review Submissions". 
    *   *MCQs are pre-graded.*
    *   Review the descriptive answers, input the marks awarded for each question, and save. The final score is automatically calculated.

### 4. Managing Assignments
*   **Creating:** Go to the Assignments tab and click "Create Assignment". Provide a title, detailed instructions, a strict deadline, and an optional reference file.
*   **Grading:** Click "Grade Submissions" on an assignment. You can download each student's submitted file, review their work, assign a grade (e.g., out of 100), and leave personalized written feedback.

### 5. Analytics
*   The **Analytics Tab** provides visual charts illustrating student enrollment trends and average grades across your assessments, helping you identify areas where students might need more support.

## 🛡️ Administrator Guide

Administrators have overarching control over the platform's structure and user base.

### 1. Institutional Structure
*   **Departments & Subjects:** Manage the core academic structure. Create new departments (e.g., Computer Science) and link subjects (e.g., Data Structures) to them. 

### 2. User & Enrollment Management
*   **Course Enrollments:** Assign faculty to specific courses and enroll students. This ensures users only see data relevant to them.
*   *(Additional user management tools are available in the expanded admin panel).*

### 3. System Settings
*   Ensure the platform is configured correctly, including managing global theme preferences (Light/Dark mode defaults).
